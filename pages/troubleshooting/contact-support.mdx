# Contact support

Three email lines, all monitored by Arc & Anchor staff (the same five people who built Rubberfit).

| For | Email |
|---|---|
| General help, bug reports, feature requests | `hello@rubberfit.app` |
| Security questions, vulnerability disclosure, security questionnaires | `security@rubberfit.app` |
| Sales, Enterprise pricing, RFPs | `sales@rubberfit.app` |

## Response time

We commit to **one business day** on all three channels. Most replies land within a few hours during U.S. business hours (Pacific Time).

## What helps us help you faster

When you write in, include:

1. Your workspace ID (visible in `/dashboard/admin/settings`)
2. The browser + OS you're seeing the issue on
3. The exact URL where the problem happens
4. A screenshot if there's a visible UI issue
5. The timestamp (UTC) — we'll match against logs

## Vulnerability disclosure

We follow standard responsible disclosure. Email `security@rubberfit.app` with the issue, expected impact, and reproduction steps. We aim to acknowledge within 1 business day and patch within 30 days for high-severity issues.

We do not currently run a paid bounty program. If your disclosure leads to a meaningful improvement, we'll thank you publicly (with permission) and credit you in the changelog.
